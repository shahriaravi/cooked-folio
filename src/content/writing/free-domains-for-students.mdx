If you're a student and you're not using the [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack), you're leaving free domains on the table. Real ones. The kind that normally cost $20 to $70 a year.

Here's what's actually worth grabbing.

## First, get approved

Apply at [education.github.com/pack](https://education.github.com/pack). You'll need:

- A school email or a photo of your student ID
- To be 13 or older
- An active GitHub account

Approval takes anywhere from a few hours to a couple of days.

## The domains worth grabbing

**`.me` via [nc.me](https://nc.me) (Namecheap)** gives you one free `.me` domain for a year. Perfect for personal sites and portfolios. This is what I use for [shahriaravi.me](https://shahriaravi.me).

**`.tech` via [get.tech](https://get.tech/github-students)** gives you one free `.tech` domain for a year. Great for technical portfolios or dev flavored projects.

**`.dev`, `.app`, `.site`, `.online`, `.studio`, `.design` via [Name.com](https://www.name.com/partner/github-students)** gives you one free domain from this list for a year. Pick whichever fits your project.

That's it. Three providers, up to three free domains. Don't grab all of them just because you can.

## The one thing nobody tells you

Every free student domain needs a **credit or debit card on file**. Auto renew is usually on by default.

**Turn off auto renew immediately** after registering. Otherwise you'll get a surprise $30 to $50 charge in twelve months. Set a calendar reminder for month eleven so you can decide whether to keep it or let it go.

## My recommendation

Get the `.me` first. It's the most versatile. If you're building something more technical, add a `.dev` or `.tech`. Skip the rest unless you have a specific project waiting for one.

Point it at Vercel or Netlify, deploy something, and you've got a real site with a real domain in under an hour.

That's how most of us started.
